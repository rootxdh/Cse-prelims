Reading the paper: the official PDFs are bilingual. English questions are on the odd-numbered pages (3, 5, 7 … 43), Hindi on the even pages. Each paper has 80 compulsory questions; there is no sectional time limit, but the whole paper must be finished in two hours.
How to attempt a paper

A 3-pass method that matches CSAT

Pass 1 · 55 minutes

Answer quant, series, syllogism, direction and blood-relation questions. These are decision-clean: once the method is right, the answer is unambiguous.

Pass 2 · 45 minutes

Data sufficiency, arrangements and puzzles. Skip any set that needs more than 90 seconds to model; return only if time remains.

Pass 3 · 20 minutes

Reading comprehension and statement-based inference. Read the questions first, then the passage. Do not chase 100% accuracy — chase 66.67% with margin.

Answer keys: UPSC publishes final answer keys after the examination cycle. For practice, use the explanations in the topic banks; if a published key differs, the official key always wins.

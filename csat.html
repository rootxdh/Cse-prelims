Section A · Basic numeracy

Quantitative Aptitude

UPSC does not ask textbook drills. It wraps Class X arithmetic inside counting, digit puzzles, data interpretation and multi-step comparisons. The banks reflect that.

Section B · Logical reasoning & analytical ability

Logical Reasoning

Data sufficiency, syllogism, conditional logic and multi-attribute puzzles dominate recent papers. The goal is not speed alone — it is knowing when the data is actually sufficient.

Practice protocol: solve 25 questions in one go, commit to an option before opening the answer, and note the reason for every mistake. The “Incorrect” filter is your revision queue.
Exam protocol: CSAT is qualifying at 66.67%. In Prelims 2022 and 2023, many serious aspirants failed Paper II — most lost marks to reasoning and time pressure, not to advanced maths.
